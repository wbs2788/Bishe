随着文生视频（Text-to-Video）扩散模型的突破性进展，生成内容的逼真度与时空连贯性大幅提升，但也引发了版权侵权、身份滥用及有害内容生成等严峻的安全挑战。现有图像级防御手段由于计算成本高、缺乏时序一致性及对隐蔽攻击拦截不足，难以直接应用于视频生成任务。为此，本文提出了一套面向文生视频模型的全流程安全防御体系，构建了“生成前置检测—生成中概念擦除—系统集成应用”的闭环框架，以实现高效、可控的安全生成。本文的主要研究内容如下：

首先，针对恶意提示词的隐蔽性与多义性，提出了基于大语言模型（LLM）思维链增强的前置安全检测方法。 本文以轻量级 Llama-3.1-8B 为基座，通过参数高效微调（PEFT）注入领域安全知识。结合“策略即提示（Policy-as-Prompt）”与思维链推理机制，引导模型生成显式推理轨迹，显著提升了对隐喻等“灰域”风险的上下文感知与逻辑判断能力，并在 WildGuard 等基准测试中有效降低了漏检率。

其次，针对模型内部潜藏的敏感概念与版权形象，提出了一种基于潜空间直接偏好优化（Latent-DPO）的视频概念擦除方法。 该方法直接在扩散模型的潜空间内构建正负偏好轨迹以约束去噪过程，避免了逐帧像素级解码，大幅降低了计算开销。同时，引入时空-语义对齐损失（STSA），通过约束交叉注意力分布，在移除目标概念的同时维持了视频的时序连贯性与非目标语义的完整性。此外，本文构建了包含 2.4 万对样本的视频概念偏好数据集 VCE-24K。实验表明，该方法在安全性指标上显著优于现有基线，并保持了极高的视频生成质量。

最后，基于上述核心算法，设计并实现了文生视频全流程安全防御系统。 该系统集成了前置检测与安全生成模块，构建了“预检—干预—评测”的三阶段安全闭环。系统采用前后端分离架构，支持实时风险拦截与安全视频展示，并在恶意攻击拦截与版权 IP 保护等典型场景中充分验证了其实用性与稳定性。

\intobmk\chapter*{Abstract}% 显示在书签但不显示在目录

With the breakthrough progress of text-to-video (T2V) diffusion models, the visual fidelity and spatio-temporal coherence of generated content have significantly improved. However, this advancement has also triggered severe safety challenges, including copyright infringement, identity misuse, and the generation of harmful content. Existing image-level defense methods are inadequate for video generation tasks due to prohibitive computational costs, a lack of temporal consistency, and insufficient capability to intercept implicit semantic attacks. To address these issues, this paper proposes a full-pipeline safety defense system tailored for T2V models. We construct a closed-loop framework of "pre-generation detection, in-generation concept erasure, and systematic integrated application" to achieve efficient and controllable safe generation. The main contributions of this paper are as follows:

First, to tackle the stealthiness and ambiguity of malicious prompts, we propose a pre-generation safety detection method enhanced by Large Language Model (LLM) Chain-of-Thought (CoT). Building upon the lightweight Llama-3.1-8B, we inject domain-specific safety knowledge via Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT). By designing a "Policy-as-Prompt" mechanism and a CoT reasoning paradigm, we guide the model to generate explicit reasoning trajectories. This significantly enhances the contextual awareness and logical judgment against "gray-area" risks such as metaphors and implications, effectively reducing the miss-detection rate on benchmarks like WildGuard.

Second, targeting the sensitive concepts and copyrighted images embedded within the model, we introduce a video concept erasure method based on Latent Direct Preference Optimization (Latent-DPO). This method constructs positive and negative preference trajectories directly within the diffusion model's latent space to constrain the denoising process, completely circumventing frame-by-frame pixel-level decoding and substantially reducing computational overhead. To prevent spatio-temporal collapse during erasure, we design a Spatio-Temporal-Semantic Alignment (STSA) loss. By constraining the context distribution in the cross-attention mechanism, STSA ensures the temporal coherence of the video and the integrity of non-target semantics while removing the target concept. Furthermore, we construct VCE-24K, a video concept preference dataset containing 24,000 sample pairs, providing a standardized benchmark for related research. Experiments demonstrate that our method significantly outperforms existing baselines in CLIPScore safety metrics while maintaining exceptional generative video quality on VBench.

Finally, based on the core algorithms above, we design and implement a comprehensive T2V safety defense system. Integrating the pre-detection and safe-generation modules, the system establishes a three-stage safety closed loop of "pre-check, intervention, and evaluation." Utilizing a decoupled frontend-backend architecture, it supports user interaction, real-time risk interception, and safe video demonstration. The practicality and stability of the system are fully validated in typical application scenarios, such as malicious attack interception and copyright IP protection.
